01 Importar certificado
Baixar o certificado (ver connect no DocumentDB)
wget https://s3.amazonaws.com/rds-downloads/rds-combined-ca-bundle.pem

Importar o certificado no DMS
aws dms import-certificate  --certificate-identifier docdb-cert  --certificate-pem file://./rds-combined-ca-bundle.pem --no-verify-ssl --profile user1

Listar o ARN do certificado
aws dms describe-certificates --output text --profile user1 --no-verify-ssl --filters Name="certificate-id",Values="rds-combined-ca-bundle" --query 'Certificates[*].CertificateArn'
